Vox

Free voice dictation and meeting notes for Windows

Hold a shortcut, speak, and cleaned-up text appears wherever your cursor is. Take meeting notes with a transcript, summary, action items and who said what. Vox runs on your PC and uses Groq's free speech and AI models with your own key.

Download for Windows Source code
Dictation. Hold Ctrl + Win to talk, release to insert. Double-tap for hands-free. Filler words removed, self-corrections applied, punctuation fixed, tone matched to the app.
Meeting notes. Records your mic and your PC's audio. No bot joins the call. Optional Google Calendar connection adds meeting titles and attendee names.
Your data stays yours. Settings, history and notes are stored on your PC. See the privacy policy.
